## Slide 1: The Problem

### Hearing Loss is a Global Health Crisis

- **466 million** people have disabling hearing loss
- **80%** of people who need hearing aids don't have access
- Average wait time for hearing test: **3-6 weeks**
- Traditional audiometer cost: **$5,000 - $15,000**

> "Hearing loss is the third most common chronic health condition in the US"
> — National Institutes of Health

## Slide 2: Our Solution

### AudioApp: Professional Audiometry, Anywhere

**One app. Three user types. Unlimited accessibility.**

| Patient | Audiologist | Administrator |
|---------|-------------|---------------|
| Self-screening | Diagnostic testing | User management |
| Results tracking | Full audiograms | Analytics |
| Education | PDF reports | Calibration |
