# Executive Summary: AudioApp

**Professional Audiometry Application for iOS & Android**

---

## Vision

AudioApp transforms hearing healthcare by providing a professional-grade audiometry solution that works on consumer devices, enabling hearing assessments in any clinical setting - from hospitals to remote telehealth consultations.

---

## The Opportunity

### Market Size
- **466 million people** worldwide have disabling hearing loss
- **Global hearing aids market**: $9.2B (2024) → $15.8B (2030)
- **Mobile health market**: $150B by 2028
- **OTC hearing aid market** (new since 2022): $1B+ annually

### The Gap
- Traditional audiometers cost **$3,000 - $15,000**
- Require **dedicated sound booths** ($10,000+)
- Limited to **clinical settings only**
- **Long wait times** for hearing assessments (weeks to months)

## Clinical Validation Approach

### Accuracy Targets

| Use Case | Target | Industry Standard |
|----------|--------|-------------------|
| **Diagnostic Mode** | ±5 dB | ANSI S3.6 requires ±3 dB |
| **Screening Mode** | ±10 dB | Acceptable for pass/fail |

### Validation Protocol
1. Partner with licensed audiologists
2. Test against calibrated reference audiometers (TDH-39)
3. 20+ participants per headphone model
4. Document 95% confidence intervals
