## 4. Regulatory Tailwinds

### 4.1 OTC Hearing Aid Rule (2022)

- FDA established OTC hearing aid category
- Removed audiologist requirement for mild-moderate loss
- Created pathway for software-based solutions

### 4.2 Apple FDA Authorization (2024)

- First OTC hearing aid SOFTWARE device approved
- Established precedent for app-based hearing solutions
- Increased consumer awareness and acceptance

### 4.3 Telehealth Expansion

- COVID accelerated acceptance of remote care
- Tele-audiology reimbursement expanding
- Remote patient monitoring codes available
