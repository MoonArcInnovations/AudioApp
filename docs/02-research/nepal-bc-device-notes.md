# Nepal BC Device Notes (Initial)

## Goal
Identify the BC (bone conduction) hardware commonly available in Nepal to prioritize integration.

## Current Evidence
Public sources do not clearly list a single dominant BC transducer brand for Nepal.
However, standard audiometer bone-conduction transducers used globally include models like RadioEar B-71 and B-81, which are commonly bundled with clinical audiometers worldwide.

Some Nepal e-commerce listings show diagnostic audiometers that include bone-conduction capability, but these listings do not prove prevalence across Nepal.

## What We Should Do Next
1. Verify commonly used audiometers/transducers via Nepal hospitals and audiology clinics.
2. Ask local distributors and biomedical suppliers for sales volume.
3. If uncertain, support the most common global transducers first (B-71/B-81) and confirm compatibility with Nepal clinics.

## Constraints
- Hardware integration depends on vendor SDKs or device interface (USB/Bluetooth).
- Calibration requirements vary by transducer model.
